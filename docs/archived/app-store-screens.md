# BAM Labs Client App – App Store Screenshot Concepts

## Core Messaging
- **Make progress feel tangible**
- **Keep your tracking with you anywhere**

---

## Screen 1: Hero / Value Proposition

**Headline:**
> Every rep satisfies.
> Every gain stacks up.

**Subhead (optional):**
> Track your workouts. See your progress. Stay consistent.

**Paired UI:**
Home screen (left variant) – Shows weekly goal progress, motivational quote, "Let's Go" CTA

**Source:** Image 1 (Home) – Left screen

**Why this pairing:** Sets the emotional tone. The motivational quote on screen ("Progress shows not just in strength but in control & clarity") reinforces the tangible progress message.

---

## Screen 2: Weekly Goals

**Headline:**
> Your week. Your goals.
> Your momentum.

**Subhead (optional):**
> Set a weekly workout goal and watch yourself crush it.

**Paired UI:**
Home screen showing weekly goal bar (2/3 workouts) and day-of-week completion tracker

**Source:** Image 1 (Home) – Left or right screen (top section focus)

**Why this pairing:** Shows the habit-building loop. Users immediately see goal progress is visual and rewarding.

---

## Screen 3: Train with Guidance

**Headline:**
> Follow the plan.
> Log every set.

**Subhead (optional):**
> Structured routines with real-time tracking for every exercise.

**Paired UI:**
Active workout screen – Shows exercise name, timer (1h 54m), sets/reps grid, weight inputs

**Source:** Image 2 (Train) – 4th screen from left (active workout with Dumbbell Tricep Extension)

**Why this pairing:** Demonstrates the core value – guided execution with live logging. Users see the app isn't just a tracker; it's a training companion.

---

## Screen 4: Pick Up Where You Left Off

**Headline:**
> Life interrupts.
> Your workout doesn't have to.

**Subhead (optional):**
> Resume any session exactly where you stopped.

**Paired UI:**
Home screen with "Current Workout" card showing elapsed time, Resume button, progress (5/10 exercises)

**Source:** Image 1 (Home) – Right screen

**Why this pairing:** Directly speaks to "keep your tracking with you anywhere." Shows flexibility – pause mid-workout, come back later.

---

## Screen 5: Track Every Gain

**Headline:**
> 10,320 lbs lifted.
> And counting.

**Subhead (optional):**
> Total volume. Personal records. All in one place.

**Paired UI:**
Track screen – History view showing total volume, workout breakdown by exercise, record stats

**Source:** Image 2 (Train) – 5th screen (completion summary with "10,320 lbs total lifted")
*Or* Image 3 (Track) – 1st screen (History tab with stats cards)

**Why this pairing:** The big number creates an emotional anchor. Progress becomes undeniable when you see the cumulative weight you've lifted.

---

## Screen 6: Celebrate Milestones

**Headline:**
> Your records.
> Your proof.

**Subhead (optional):**
> Personal bests tracked and ready to share.

**Paired UI:**
Track screen – Records view showing personal records (Record Rep Set, Record Sets/Workout, Record Total Volume, Single Rep Max) with yearly workout visualization grid

**Source:** Image 3 (Track) – 4th screen (Records tab with personal records list and 2025 workout dots)

**Why this pairing:** Closes the loop – progress isn't just tracked, it's celebrated. The "Share Progress" feature in nearby screens reinforces shareability.

## App Store Metadata Suggestions

**App Name:**
BAM Labs

**Subtitle (30 chars max):**
Track Every Rep. See Every Gain.

**Promotional Text (170 chars):**
Your workouts, tracked. Your progress, proven. BAM Labs helps you stay consistent with guided routines, real-time logging, and a complete record of every gain you've made.

**Keywords:**
workout tracker, gym log, weight training, fitness progress, strength training, personal records, workout journal, lifting tracker

---

## Notes for Design

1. **Consistent framing** – All screenshots should use the same device frame and background treatment
2. **Headline placement** – Top third of the image, above the phone mockup
3. **Subhead optional** – Use only if space allows without crowding
4. **Badge emphasis** – Consider adding "Track," "Train," "Home" badges to help users understand navigation
5. **Dark theme advantage** – The app's dark UI will stand out against App Store's white background
